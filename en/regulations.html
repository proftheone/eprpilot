New EU rules for packaging: design requirements, recyclability, declarations of conformity and labelling. Applies from 12 August 2026.

  • SKU status

CBAM

coming soon

Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (EU) 2023/956

Importers of steel, aluminium, cement, fertilisers, hydrogen and electricity from outside the EU report embedded emissions and settle CBAM certificates.

  • Authorised declarant status
  • Supplier emissions data
  • Annual declaration

KOBiZE

coming soon

Polish national emissions database

An annual report to the Polish national emissions database for companies that use the environment: boilers, vehicle fleets, production processes.

  • Annual report
  • Aggregated emissions data
  • Deadline reminders

Deposit return system in Poland

Obligations for companies placing beverages in PET bottles, cans and reusable glass on the market, the operator contract and reporting.

  • Choosing an operator
  • Packaging labelling
  • Reports to the operator

SUP

coming soon

Single-Use Plastics Directive

Fees and obligations for single-use plastic products: cups, food containers, wet wipes, filters.

  • Product fee
  • Labelling
  • Reporting

Batteries

coming soon

Batteries Regulation (EU) 2023/1542

New battery requirements: carbon footprint, recycled content, supply chain due diligence and the battery passport.

  • Battery passport
  • Due diligence

Ecodesign and the digital product passport

Regulation (EU) 2024/1781: ecodesign requirements and the digital product passport, starting with textiles, steel, furniture and electronics.
